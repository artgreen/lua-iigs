Lua 5.4.6 for the Apple IIgs - standard distribution

Extract with GS ShrinkIt or copy from the ProDOS transfer image.
Use an ORCA-compatible shell in the installed directory:
  lua -E -v
  lua -E hello.lua
  lua -E hwsmoke.lua
  lua -E -i
Leave the interactive prompt with os.exit().

LUA is the full interpreter; LUAC compiles bytecode.
All thirteen standalone Lua examples are included. Try:
  lua -E adventure.lua
  lua -E blackjack.lua
  lua -E maze.lua 42 solve
  lua -E life.lua 6
  lua -E queens.lua 8
  lua -E warehouse.lua
This is not a boot disk or Finder application.
See EXAMPLES.TXT for the full catalog, and INSTALL.TXT, USAGE.TXT,
and COMPAT.TXT for installation, commands, and limitations.
